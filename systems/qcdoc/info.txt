These were several systems with 14,000 processors total, 1750GB memory, and peak performance of 11 TFLOPS. The largest of these was hosted at EPCC's Advanced Computing Facility (ACF) and had 12,228 processors and 10 TFLOPS performance. It was purpose built for simulating particle physics.  The processors on QCDOC were the forerunners of the technology used in the IBM BlueGene series of supercomputers.
