Tesseract was part of the DiRAC  UK national HPC service for particle physics, cosmology and astronomy. It was built by HPE to have a high level of interconnectivity between low power Intel CPU cores to meet the requirements of particle physics research community. At its largest, it had 1468 CPU nodes, each with two 12-core Intel CPUs and a small number of GPU nodes. It was the successor to the BlueGene/Q system and was eventually replaced by the Tursa system.
