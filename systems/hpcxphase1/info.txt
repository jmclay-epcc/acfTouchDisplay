HPCx was a UK national service run by EPCC, Daresbury Labs, and supplied by IBM, with the system located in Daresbury. It cost £50million, weighed 75 tonnes, and was about the size of a tennis court. Initially, HPCx had 1280 processors in 40 cabinets, with 1280GB memory and 6.7TFLOPS peak performace. Upgrades in 2004 and 2005 increased this to 10.9 TFLOPS and 15.4 TFLOPS.  HPCx was followed by HECToR as the UK National Supercomputing Service but the two systems ran in parallel for over a year providing different capabilities for UK researchers.
