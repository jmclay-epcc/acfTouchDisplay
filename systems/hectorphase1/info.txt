HECToR, which stands for: High-End Computing Terascale Resource. Terascale means it was capable of performance in the TFLOPS range. Like HPCx, HECToR had several upgrades in its lifetime. Phase 1 Cray XT4 was the world's 17th fastest supercomputer in its first year, with 11,328 cores and 63.4 TFLOPS peak performance. Each two-core processor had 6GB memory, so total system memory was 33TB. After the final upgrade in 2011, Cray XE6, core count had increased to 90,112 and performance to 829 TFLOPS. Each processor now had 16 cores and 16GB memory.  It was eventually replaced by ARCHER as the UK National Supercomputing Service.
