This was one of the third generation of BlueGenes, and like its predecessor L, it was fast and power efficient. It was one of several systems that made up the DiRAC service at that time - Distributed Research Utilising Advanced Computing. Having different types of systems available in the service gives the users more choice, as different architectures are better suited to different problems. This system had 6144 nodes each with an 18 core processor, and had a peak performance of 1.26 PFLOPS, making it the 20th fastest system in the world in June 2012. Water cooling was used to keep the system at a safe temperature.
