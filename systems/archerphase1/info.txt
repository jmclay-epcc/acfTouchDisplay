ARCHER, or Advanced Research Computing High-End Resource, replaced HECToR as the UK's national supercomputer, and was the service before ARCHER2. It was supplied by HPE Cray (back then they were just Cray!) and hosted and managed by EPCC. It received an upgrade in 2014 with 10 new cabinets added, increasing its performance by 60%. It was 26 cabinets with 4920 nodes, each node had two processors (24 cores total) and 64GB memory in most nodes - there were a few with 128GB. In total, the system had 118,080 cores, and peak performance was 2.55 PFLOPS.
