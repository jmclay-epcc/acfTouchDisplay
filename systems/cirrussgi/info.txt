Initially Cirrus was a system dedicated for use by industry, but its user base has grown to include academia too. It is one of several, smaller research systems across the UK (Tier-2 sites) complementary to ARCHER2 (the Tier-1 facility). It has 280 standard compute nodes, each with 36 cores and 256GB memory. An upgrade in 2020 added 36 GPU nodes, each with GPU cards. GPUs, or graphical processing units, are so-called as their primary use is for high-speed rendering of graphics in systems designed to run video games. These characteristics also make GPUs well suited for large-scale numerical codes, and they are frequently used to speed up machine learning and other data intensive processes.
