Tursa is the current Extreme Scaling component of the DiRAC  UK national service for particle physics, cosmology and astronomy. The system is supplied by Atos and consists of 114 nodes, each with four Nvidia A100 GPU all connected by an extremely high bandwidth interconnect to enable modelling of the subatomic collisions that take place at CERN, such as the LHC (Large Hadron Collider). It initially ran alongside the Tesseract system before replacing it fully when Tesseract was decommissioned in 2023.
